# 02 Solar-shading strategy

[← Back to README](../README.md)

| Condition | Blind action |
|---|---|
| **Cooling season** and sun on the façade and room above comfort | Close or angle to block direct sun |
| **Heating season** and sun on the façade and room below setpoint | Open: free solar gain |
| **Night, heating season** | Close: reduce heat loss through the glazing |
| **Glare** (occupied, low sun angle) | Partial close |
| **Wind / storm warning** | Raise or retract per manufacturer guidance ⟨check the blind type⟩ |
| **Manual override** | Hold for a timeout, then return to automatic |

**Inputs:** sun azimuth/elevation (HA sun), façade orientation per window ⟨record⟩, room temperature and setpoint, the HVAC mode, outdoor temperature, lux.
